Why this research matters

As with many technologies, AI may bring both potential benefits and potential risks. Rather than assuming that it is either entirely beneficial or entirely harmful, scientific research can help us understand both sides more clearly.

By gathering evidence, research can help identify when AI may improve people's lives, when it may create difficulties, and which individuals or situations may benefit most.

Helping people make informed choices

Ultimately, one important role of science is to provide people with reliable information that can help them make better-informed choices about their own lives.

The goal is not to tell people that they must use AI—or that they must avoid it—but to better understand its opportunities and risks so that people can make those decisions for themselves.

Your contribution matters

By participating in this study, you have already contributed to that process. Even when a study produces uncertain, mixed, or preliminary findings, the information participants provide helps researchers understand what should be investigated next and how future studies can be improved.

Your participation therefore becomes part of the wider effort to understand how emerging technologies may affect people and society.
